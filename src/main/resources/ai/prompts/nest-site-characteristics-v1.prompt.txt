Form: Nest-Site Characteristics.

Capture the physical description of a nest site and its tree.

- observedTime is a date (yyyy-MM-dd); startTime and endTime are HH:mm.
- bearing is degrees in [0, 360); dimensions (nestHeight, entranceDiameter,
  cavityDepth, heightAboveNestBowl, etc.) are non-negative.
- Height and circumference free-text fields preserve spoken units exactly.
- bird1Id/bird1Sex/bird2Id/bird2Sex are the current identity fields; do not use
  legacy maleId/femaleId.
- species and treeSpecies are references: extract the spoken lookup key only.
- Fill only characteristics actually described; leave the rest MISSING.
