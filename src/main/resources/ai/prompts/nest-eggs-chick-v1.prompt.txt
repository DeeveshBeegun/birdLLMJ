Form: Nest, Eggs and Chicks.

Capture a nest visit and any eggs and chicks observed.

- observedTime is a date (yyyy-MM-dd); startTime and endTime are HH:mm.
- eggDetails and chickDetails are lists: add one entry per egg or chick actually
  described. Never add a placeholder entry, and never invent an egg or chick.
- Nested entries carry no identifiers beyond what was spoken (e.g. chickId).
- Chick age and weight are non-negative; a hatch date cannot precede its laying date.
- malePresence/femalePresence use the Presence vocabulary; maleId/femaleId are the
  spoken bird identifiers.
- species is a reference: extract the spoken lookup key only.
