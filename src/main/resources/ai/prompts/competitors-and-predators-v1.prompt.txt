Form: Competitors and Predators.

Capture competing or predatory species observed at or near a nest.

- observedTime is a date (yyyy-MM-dd).
- numOfCompetitor and distanceFromNest are non-negative.
- Fill competitor fields only when consistent with hasCompetitorInfo; if no
  competitor information was described, leave them MISSING.
- competitorSpecies and species are references: extract the spoken lookup key only.
- impact and action are lists of vocabulary values; include only stated items.
- Mark conflicting presence statements as AMBIGUOUS rather than guessing.
