Form: Feeding Observation.

Capture what a bird was feeding on and the surrounding conditions.

- observedTime is a date (yyyy-MM-dd), not a time.
- Fill feeding fields (itemConsumed, itemDescription, feedingOutCome) only if
  feeding was actually described. Do not fabricate feeding details.
- Resolve treeSpecies only if a tree is referenced; treeStatus likewise.
- foragingHeight and treeHeight are free text: preserve the spoken phrasing and
  units exactly; do not convert.
- Weather (cloud, wind, rain) only if stated; otherwise leave MISSING.
- species and ringingMorphs are references: extract the spoken lookup key only.
- The property name feedingOutCome is intentional; do not rename it.
